Vice City Cover Maker — a web app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art, exported as a PNG to share. Five photo slots with drag-to-pan and scroll-to-zoom, four multi-panel layouts (Neon Grid, Hero, Split, Strip), four colour grades (Leonida Heat, Neon Night, Teal & Orange, Gold Noir), a bottom title band with a code-drawn skyline and a big Anton caption, toggleable "EST. 2026" starburst and "NEW" badges, and one-click PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Three preset covers ship with it: NEON PURSUIT (wide), SUNSET HEIST (square) and BOULEVARD QUEEN (portrait), all starring a classic car chased by a police helicopter. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser — no network request, photos never leave your machine. Original art only: no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts; Anton and Oswald are SIL Open Font License.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8901
```
